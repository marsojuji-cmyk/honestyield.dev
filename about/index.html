Why this exists

Every week, someone claims their AI system saves tokens, money, or time. Almost none of those claims can be checked. Different workloads, unstated conditions, vanished baselines — the numbers are marketing wearing a lab coat.

Honest yield is the refusal. A yield claim is a measured claim: a before-and-after run pair on the same workload, under stated conditions, published where anyone can read it — including the pairs that came back flat. The standard is small on purpose. Six clauses, one ledger, no exceptions.
